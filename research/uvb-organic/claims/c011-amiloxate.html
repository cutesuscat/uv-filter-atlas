Amiloxate is a structural isomer of octinoxate — same p-methoxycinnamate chromophore, isoamyl ester instead of 2-ethylhexyl. Spectroscopically very close to octinoxate.

Photodegradation

  • Photostability: rated moderate-to-poor (cinnamate class). Like octinoxate, undergoes trans → cis photoisomerization to a less-absorbing cis isomer at PSS, plus [2+2] photodimerization in concentrated form. Quantum yields of trans → cis are likely in the same 0.20–0.40 range as OMC. C2 — by analogy; specific primary measurement not located.
  • In combination with avobenzone, expected to undergo the same [2+2] cycloaddition reported for OMC+BMDBM (Sayre 2005) — C3 by mechanistic analogy; not directly studied.
  • Photoproducts: cis-amiloxate (Z-isomer); diester truxillic-type photodimer; trace 4-methoxybenzaldehyde — C3 by analogy to OMC photochemistry (Hanson et al. 2015).
  • C5 — direct primary photodegradation kinetic measurements not located in literature search.

Notable

  • Amiloxate is essentially an OMC variant. Its commercial appeal is largely the Symrise supply chain and a marginally different INCI label; photochemically very similar.
  • Has been on the FDA TEA pending list since 2003 with no sign of imminent approval.
  • Endocrine concern by structural analogy to octinoxate — likely subject to the same SCCS scrutiny if reviewed.
  • Skin penetration: detectable in plasma in human max-use trials (Matta-style FDA studies); concentrations comparable to OMC.
