## Regulatory documents

* **SCCS/1429/11**, revision adopted 13–14 Dec 2011: Opinion on 1,3,5-Triazine, 2,4,6-tris[1,1'-biphenyl]-4-yl- (TBPT, Tinosorb A2B). European Commission, Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety.
* **SCCS/1605/19**, final opinion adopted 13 Dec 2019: Methoxypropylamino Cyclohexenylidene Ethoxyethylcyanoacetate (S87) — Submission II. European Commission, SCCS.
* **Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/1684** (12 Nov 2020): amending Annex VI to Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 — adds MCE at 3% max.
* **Council Directive 76/768/EEC** as amended; later **Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009** Annex VI — current EU UV filter positive list.
* **US FDA. Drugs@FDA, NDA 21-502** (Anthelios SX moisturizer with sunscreen). Approved 24 July 2006.
* **US FDA. Proposed Administrative Order OTC000039**: Amending Over-the-Counter Monograph M020 (Sunscreen Drug Products for OTC Human Use) — proposes bemotrizinol GRASE at 6%. Federal Register Doc. 2025-22649, published 12 Dec 2025.
* **US FDA. TEA (Time and Extent Application) docket FDA-2005-N-0453** for bemotrizinol; original submission April 2005.
* **US Sunscreen Innovation Act, Pub. L. 113-195**, signed 26 Nov 2014.
