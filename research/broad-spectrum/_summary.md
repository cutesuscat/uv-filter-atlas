# Broad-Spectrum / Mexoryl-Class UV Filters — Comparative Summary

> Created 2026-05-07. Companion to claim files c001–c006 in `claims/`.
> All "next-generation" organic UV filters: highly photostable, broad-spectrum or deep-UVA, large MW, negligible skin penetration. Most are EU-approved but **NOT** US-approved (FDA's TEA backlog).

## Spectral coverage map (peak wavelengths)

```
   UVB  |---- UVA2 ----|---- UVA1 ----|
   290           320            340     360         400 nm
   |             |              |       |           |
   ●═════════════●═══════════════════════════════════  Bemotrizinol (BEMT) 310, 340
                          ●══════════════════════════  Ecamsule 345
   ●═════════════●═══════════════════════════════════  Drometrizole Trisiloxane 303, 344
                                            ●═════════ Mexoryl 400 (MCE) 385
   ●═════════[scattering]═══════════════════════════   Tinosorb A2B 310 + scatter
                  ●══════════════════════════════════  Bisdisulizole 335
```

**Note on coverage gaps that motivate the modern filter blends:**

* Pre-2019 filters left a gap above ~370 nm (the "ultra-long UVA1" band that drives photoaging and pigmentation). MCE (Mexoryl 400) at 385 nm closes that gap.
* No single broad-spectrum filter covers 280–400 nm uniformly. Modern SPF50+ formulae stack 4–7 filters: typically a UVB workhorse (octinoxate/octocrylene/ethylhexyl triazone), a UVA1 anchor (avobenzone/DHHB/MCE), a broadband (BEMT/MBBT/TBPT), and often inorganics.
* Two filters in this list are primarily *single-peak* UVA2 (ecamsule, bisdisulizole). They MUST be combined with a UVB filter to make any SPF claim.

## Solubility / phase compatibility

| Phase | Filters in this set |
|---|---|
| Oil phase (lipophilic) | Bemotrizinol, Drometrizole Trisiloxane, Mexoryl 400 |
| Water phase (hydrophilic) | Ecamsule (as TEA/disodium salt), Bisdisulizole |
| Particulate (dispersed in water phase) | Tinosorb A2B |

This matters for formulators: a high-SPF, broad-spectrum formula must distribute filters across all phases to maintain a continuous protective film.

## Photostability ranking (qualitative, decreasing)

1. **Drometrizole trisiloxane (Mexoryl XL)** — ESIPT class, virtually indestructible.
2. **Bemotrizinol (Tinosorb S)** — ESIPT-like, 98.4% at 50 MED; photostabilizes avobenzone.
3. **Tinosorb A2B (TBPT)** — particulate; sub-ps IC; no characterized photoproducts.
4. **Bisdisulizole (Neo Heliopan AP)** — rigid bis-benzimidazole; >95% retention.
5. **MCE (Mexoryl 400)** — sponsor data "100% intrinsic stability"; awaits independent verification.
6. **Ecamsule (Mexoryl SX)** — 94–99% (Loréal/Seite data); some independent studies report 60% at very high doses; reversible isomerization.

For comparison: avobenzone alone retains ~36% after 1 h sun; oxybenzone ~80%; octinoxate ~80%.
