## Bias and limitation notes

- Many efficacy claims (esp. for DEHN, DESM, Polycrylene, Tinosorb M) originate in manufacturer technical literature and trade-press articles. These are flagged C2 with explicit bias caveats in each claim file.
- The "Huong et al. 2008 J Photochem Photobiol A" reference cited in the topic prompt could not be verified by direct PubMed/ScienceDirect search; the most plausible reference fitting that description is Mturi & Martincigh 2008 *J Photochem Photobiol A* 200:410-420 (sourced repeatedly above).
- Some primary kq (triplet quenching rate constant) numbers reported in claim files (~10⁹ M⁻¹s⁻¹) are extracted from the Lhiaubet-Vallet 2010 abstract and Paris 2009 — full-text values may differ in detail; the order of magnitude is well-established.
- Cross-study quantitative AVB residual percentages should be interpreted as directional. Differences in irradiation source, MED definition, dose, vehicle, and quantitation method limit absolute comparability.
