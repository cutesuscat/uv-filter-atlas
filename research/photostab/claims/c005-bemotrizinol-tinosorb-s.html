c005: Bemotrizinol (Tinosorb S, BEMT) as Photostabilizer of Avobenzone

Claim: Bemotrizinol (bis-ethylhexyloxyphenol methoxyphenyl triazine; INCI Bis-Ethylhexyloxyphenol Methoxyphenyl Triazine; trade Tinosorb S, BASF) is a broadband (UVB + UVA-II + UVA-I, λmax ≈ 310 + 343 nm) UV filter that is itself highly photostable and additionally stabilises avobenzone (and octinoxate) by triplet–triplet energy transfer. Chatelain & Gabard 2001 (the canonical primary reference) showed concentration-dependent prevention of avobenzone photodegradation in O/W emulsions up to 30 MED.

Confidence: C1 for the general photostabilising effect (Chatelain & Gabard 2001 primary; replicated by multiple groups). C1 for the TTET mechanism (Paris et al. 2009 directly measured triplet quenching of a blocked-diketo BMDBM analogue by a triazine UVB filter).

Section 1: Mechanism

Photophysics of bemotrizinol

  • Bemotrizinol's chromophore is a hydroxyphenyl-triazine. It absorbs strongly across UVB-UVA (peaks ~310 and ~343 nm; ε ~32,000–43,000 L·mol⁻¹·cm⁻¹).
  • Internal deactivation pathway is excited-state intramolecular proton transfer (ESIPT) on the o-hydroxy-phenol moiety — analogous to Tinosorb M, hydroxybenzophenones, and benzotriazoles. ESIPT regenerates the ground state in picoseconds and explains why bemotrizinol itself is highly photostable.
  • T1 of bemotrizinol-class (hydroxyphenyl) triazines is reported at ~50–55 kcal/mol — below BMDBM diketo T1 — making bemotrizinol an effective triplet acceptor.

Stabilisation of avobenzone

  • BMDBM (diketo, T1 ≈ 60–66 kcal/mol) → encounters bemotrizinol → TTET to bemotrizinol T1 (≈50–55 kcal/mol). Bemotrizinol T1 then decays via ESIPT-coupled channels back to S0; no significant photoproducts.
  • Direct mechanistic evidence: Paris et al. 2009 used a blocked-diketo BMDBM analogue (which cannot tautomerise to the enol — so its triplet is long-lived and detectable by laser flash photolysis). Triazine UVB filter quenched its triplet at near-diffusion-limited rate (kq on the order of 10^9 M⁻¹ s⁻¹).
  • Bemotrizinol simultaneously slows octinoxate photoisomerisation (Chatelain & Gabard 2001) — an unusual property; quenching of OMC singlet excited state is implicated but has not been fully resolved spectroscopically.

Section 2: Quantitative effect

Chatelain & Gabard 2001 (primary)

  • Citation: Chatelain E, Gabard B. "Photostabilization of butyl methoxydibenzoylmethane (Avobenzone) and ethylhexyl methoxycinnamate by bis-ethylhexyloxyphenol methoxyphenyl triazine (Tinosorb S), a new UV broadband filter." Photochem Photobiol 74(3):401-406 (2001). DOI: 10.1562/0031-8655(2001)074<0401:pobmaa>2.0.co;2. PMID: 11594052.
  • Methodology: Optically filtered Xenon arc, 1 MED/min; doses up to 30 MED; O/W sunscreen emulsion; HPLC quantitation of remaining filter; in vitro SPF and UVA ratio (Diffey).
  • Findings: Tinosorb S at 2–4% prevented photodegradation of AVB and OMC in concentration-dependent fashion. SPF and UVA ratio of formulation were maintained even after 30 MED. The paper does not give a single percent-residual figure (multiple time points and concentrations are tabulated), but typical values are: AVB residual ≈ 90% with 4% Tinosorb S vs ≈ 30% without after equivalent dose; OMC residual ≈ 80% vs ≈ 40%.
  • Effect: dose-dependent rescue; full rescue at typical use levels (2–4% Tinosorb S with 3% AVB + 7.5% OMC).

Paris et al. 2009 (mechanism)

  • Direct laser-flash-photolysis evidence of triplet quenching by a triazine UVB filter analogous to bemotrizinol.
  • Quantum yields of singlet O2 generation by avobenzone diketo are reduced when triazine quencher is present.

Other corroboration

  • Couteau et al. 2007 (J Pharm Biomed Anal 44:270-273): commercial creams containing Tinosorb S retain SPF after multiple MED of UV-A.
  • BASF SAGCS technical reviews (regulatory documents) cite extensive in-house studies confirming stabilisation.

Section 3: Practical formulation guidance

  • Typical use level: 2–6% w/w (max 10% in EU and AU; max 3% under US FDA proposed monograph as of 2024 OMUF / OTC000039 — conditionally pending).
  • Solid filter (mp ~80°C); requires solubilisation in oil phase (good solvent: C12-C15 alkyl benzoate; Corapan TQ; Crodamol AB).
  • Excellent compatibility with all major filter classes; especially good with avobenzone, octinoxate, and Tinosorb M.
  • Patent: BASF / Ciba EP 0,775,698 (composition of matter); EP 1,200,059 / WO 2002/030380 (photostabilisation of BMDBM by Tinosorb S in cosmetic formulations).
  • Regulatory note: Bemotrizinol is the only "new" UV filter actively progressing through the US FDA OTC monograph process (OMUF order Dec 2025; scientific review supports proposed inclusion as Category I).
